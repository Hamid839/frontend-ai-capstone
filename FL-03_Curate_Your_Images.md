---

## 4. Rejection note (discernment)

**Rejected:** a glossy AI-generated hero with dramatic lighting, fake glass panels, glowing nodes, and several competing accent colors.

**Why:** It would become the most memorable thing on the page. A reviewer would remember the decoration, not the MedNoviAI appointment UI or FocusBoard workspace. It also had the common “AI slop” look that makes portfolios look interchangeable.

**What I chose instead:** the two real screenshots above, placed in a calm frame (near-white canvas, forest-green accents from the identity kit). The work is the colorful, memorable part — not a generated background.

This is the judgment the assignment asks for: the rejected image fights the purpose of the portfolio; the keepers serve the proof.

---

## 5. Screenshot handling rules applied

- Captured from the real applications
- Full useful UI context retained (navigation + main content)
- No private secrets or credentials shown
- Not replaced by AI reconstructions
- Not presented as something I did not build or contribute to

## 6. Consistency with the identity kit

- Screenshots remain the visual interest; the page frame stays quiet
